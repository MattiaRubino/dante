## Construction contract

Master v0 is a **hybrid 2D production master**.

- Canvas: `1536 × 1536`, transparent.
- The render layer links the canonical `1254 × 1254` approved PNG at `x=141`, `y=141`, `width=1254`, `height=1254`; no scaling, redraw, recolor or restyle is introduced by the master.
- The SVG contains a separate non-rendering vector construction-guide group with silhouette and key structural anchors.
- The reference PNG and SVG master are one production package: the PNG is surface/render authority; the SVG is framing and construction authority.
- The vector guides support controlled downstream framing, pose planning and reconstruction work; they do not replace the approved rendered surface.

This structure is intentional. The approved watercolor/cosmic texture is raster information; calling a traced approximation a fully editable vector master would be misleading and could alter the accepted character.

## Derivative rules

Allowed after separate bounded scope:

- avatar crops/framing;
- resolution/export derivatives;
- simplified UI treatments traceable to this master;
- activity/state poses built from the locked identity;
- later pixel-retro derivative.

Not allowed as ordinary derivative work:

- regenerate or redesign the base mascot;
- alter its silhouette, face or orange/cosmic identity treatment;
- add a second tail;
- replace the small tail sun/orbit language with a different dominant emblem;
- add the mascot to the primary DANTE signature by default.
