## Visual contract

- symbol remains visually dominant but not oversized;
- DANTE remains clearly legible and secondary to the symbol;
- components sit side-by-side;
- vertical relationship is optical centering, not baseline alignment;
- spacing is open but controlled;
- no tagline, `AI` descriptor, mascot, glow, texture or added decoration;
- symbol colors remain `#222F37` and `#EA5C12`;
- wordmark remains `#222F37` in the primary light-background signature.

## Production rule

Normal exports must derive from this signature or from the two locked component masters. Do not rebuild the lockup by retyping `DANTE`, regenerating the symbol, or approximating spacing by eye when an exact asset is available.

Dark/inverse, monochrome, stacked, compact and platform-specific signatures are separate derivatives and require their own validation if created.

## Approval history

Several exploratory compositions were reviewed. Baseline-aligned variants were rejected. The workstream reset to the original Balanced+ optical-centering composition, which was then explicitly approved for save on 2026-08-18.
