## Identity

```text
Contributor = contextual Actor role
Contribution != native entity/root
```

No Person, Account, Collective or Actor identity is manufactured by the relation.

## Authority

```text
Contribution != Authority
Contribution != Visibility
Contribution != Consent
Contribution != ownership
```

Attribution grants no governance or access capability by default.

## Reality/history

```text
Contribution != Actual
Contribution != Outcome
Contribution != Provenance
```

Shared Actual/Outcome remains shared; actor-specific Contribution can change under correction without silently rewriting correctly recorded shared reality. Consequential historical attribution remains reconstructible.

## Relationships / Reasoning

Contribution survives as a specific relation family, but specific truthful relations remain preferred when complete. Contribution is not a universal collaboration/relationship wrapper.

## Multi-Actor

Several Actors may contribute differently to one bounded context without becoming a Collective. A true Collective may bear Contribution where the attribution truthfully belongs to that referent; member/Collective attribution does not propagate automatically.

## Language

Reserve capitalized `Contribution` for the accepted actor-scoped relation. Generic phrases such as “Activity contributes to Goal” remain evaluative/planning language unless the actor-scoped semantics are actually intended.
