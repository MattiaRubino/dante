## Identity

Ownership/Possession are relations around native referents; they do not manufacture Person, Asset, Collective, Actor, Account, Subject or Resource identity. Owner/Possessor/Custodian are contextual roles.

## Authority

```text
Ownership != Authority
Possession != Authority
Custody != Authority
```

Applicable governance may depend on ownership/custody facts, but the effect remains owned by Authority/policy semantics.

## Current/history/material state

Current owner/possessor may differ from historical state. Consequential correction is non-destructive; later relation change does not silently rewrite prior truthful state.

## Relationships / Reasoning

Ownership and Possession survive as specific relation families. Custody survives only as bounded composition/profile. No universal Property/Control/Stewardship wrapper is accepted.

## Multi-Actor

Multiple owners/possessors do not automatically form a Collective. Collective/member ownership and possession do not propagate automatically in either direction.

## Language

Reserve capitalized `Ownership` and `Possession` for the accepted relation semantics. Use `Custody` for the bounded entrusted/safeguarding profile, not as a synonym for ownership, possession, responsibility or stewardship.
