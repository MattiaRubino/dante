# 2026-08-16 — Content Artifact boundary

Content Artifact / Document v0 confirms that accepted Asset identity remains bounded to individually tracked non-human **physical** objects.

Canonical separation:

```text
Asset
= scoped native physical-object identity

Content Artifact
= scoped native information-content referent

Asset != Content Artifact
```

A physical medium can be an Asset while the information it carries is a distinct Content Artifact where both identities materially matter.

Examples:

```text
physical notebook
→ may be Asset

notes written/scanned from it
→ may be Content Artifact

USB drive
→ may be Asset

PDF stored on it
→ not that Asset's identity
```

A file/blob/provider object is not promoted into Asset merely because it is individually addressable in storage.

Content Artifact may independently play Subject or Resource role where truthful; that does not change Asset boundaries.

No digital-asset/IP/property ontology is accepted by this amendment.

Asset v0 remains **PASS WITH HARDENING; REOPEN = 0**.
