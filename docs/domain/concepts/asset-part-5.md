# 2026-08-16 — Asset ↔ Living Referent closure

Asset previously excluded animals/plants and explicitly deferred native living identity until concrete LifeOS workflows required it. That trigger has now fired.

Accepted resolution:

```text
Asset
= scoped native identity for individually tracked non-human physical objects

Living Referent
= scoped native identity for individually tracked non-human living organisms/specimens

Asset != Living Referent
```

The shared existence of persistent identity, history, location, ownership, possession, observations, responsibility or Resource use does not collapse the two native identity families.

Canonical guardrails:

```text
living organism/specimen != Asset merely because it is owned or managed
Asset != Living Referent merely because it has lifecycle/condition history
same container/owner/location != same organism identity
Living Referent may relate to an Asset without either absorbing the other
```

Examples:

```text
specific camera body -> Asset
specific dog -> Living Referent
specific bonsai -> Living Referent when individual identity materially matters
plant pot -> possible Asset when independently tracked
plant in that pot -> distinct Living Referent
```

The previous `living things` deferral is therefore resolved, not broadened into a universal `ManagedObject` or `Thing` root.
