# 2026-08-15 — Resource Allocation authority boundary

Resource Requirement / Allocation v0 confirms:

```text
Resource Allocation != Authority
```

Selecting/designating a provider does not establish who had the legitimate power to make that shared/canonical planning state effective.

Where governance applies:

```text
Decision / policy / operation
+ applicable Authority
→ effective Resource Allocation state owned by Resource Allocation semantics
```

The same Actor may propose, request, decide, record and allocate in a simple personal context, but those coincidences are not domain equivalences.

Allocating a Person does not create Authority over that Person, Responsibility, Participation, Agreement or Consent.

AI may perform a bounded Allocation only under explicit applicable policy/Authority. Optimization quality, candidate rank or access to private context never creates Authority.

A later revocation/change of Authority does not rewrite truthful historical Allocation or the Authority basis that applied at the time where material.
