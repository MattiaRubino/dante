# 2026-08-16 — Content Artifact / Possibility boundary

Possibility v0 introduces candidate-future posture. Content Artifact continues to own persistent reusable information-content identity.

Canonical separation:

```text
Content Artifact
= which persistent reusable information-bearing content item is this?

Possibility
= which materially retained candidate future remains open before adoption/decision/execution?

Content Artifact != Possibility
```

A note may contain:

```text
"Maybe spend a month in Japan"
```

but the existence of that text does not itself establish that LifeOS owns a retained Possibility. Conversely, a Possibility need not require one dedicated note/document.

Cardinality is independent:

```text
one Artifact may describe/source several Possibilities
one Possibility may be informed by several Artifacts
same text/title != same Possibility automatically
```

## Idea vocabulary

`Idea` does not become a new universal kernel root.

Depending on actual use:

```text
idea as reusable retained information/content
→ Content Artifact may suffice

idea as candidate future retained for consideration
→ Possibility may apply

idea intentionally adopted as desired outcome
→ Goal may apply
```

This classification preserves natural product language without overloading Artifact identity.

## Extraction / AI boundary

```text
AI extracts "possible trip to Japan" from Artifact
!= canonical Possibility automatically

Artifact contains preference-like statement
!= confirmed permanent preference automatically
```

Authorized product behavior may surface or retain a Possibility with appropriate provenance. Extraction remains attributable and must not manufacture user intent/adoption.

## History / representation boundary

A material Artifact revision does not automatically replace a Possibility, and a Possibility revision/reconsideration does not automatically create a new Artifact.

```text
Version(Artifact) != Version(Possibility) universally
```

Where one materially depends on the other, the applicable historical state must remain reconstructible under accepted Version/Provenance discipline.

Content Artifact v0 remains **PASS WITH HARDENING; REOPEN = 0**. Blob/storage/OCR/indexing and legal-document specialist boundaries remain unchanged.
