# 2026-08-16 — Collective Decision / Quorum integration

Collective v0 resolves the previously deferred pressure around collective decision formation without making Decision a voting engine.

A Collective may be the bounded referent whose Decision is established under applicable governance/context.

```text
Collective C
→ Decision D
```

But individual member stance remains independent:

```text
member response / stance
!= Collective Decision automatically

Collective Decision
!= every member personally agreed
```

## Quorum boundary

Quorum is bounded eligibility/threshold evaluation vocabulary.

```text
eligible set
+
threshold Criterion
+
Evaluation
→ quorum assessment
```

Therefore:

```text
quorum satisfied
!= Decision
!= Approval automatically
!= Agreement
!= Consent
!= Authority
```

Applicable governance/policy/Authority may specify that a satisfied quorum plus particular responses is sufficient basis for a Decision or effect, but the quorum assessment itself does not manufacture the Decision.
