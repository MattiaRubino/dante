# 2026-08-16 — Content Artifact boundary

Content Artifact / Document v0 confirms the accepted Evidence distinction between source information and evaluative use.

Canonical boundary:

```text
Content Artifact != Evidence
```

A Content Artifact may play Evidence role when its content or an applicable material state materially bears on a specific evaluation target/context.

The same Artifact may therefore be:

```text
Evidence for evaluation A
irrelevant to evaluation B
contradictory Evidence in evaluation C
```

without changing Artifact identity.

Where an evaluation or consequential Decision depends materially on content, the applicable Artifact material state must remain reconstructible according to Version/Evidence history rules.

Presence of a statement inside an Artifact does not establish evidentiary truth, strength or relevance automatically. AI/OCR extraction from an Artifact does not manufacture Evidence relevance or canonical fact status.

No universal `EvidenceDocument` entity/root is accepted.
