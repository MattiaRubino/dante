# 2026-08-16 — Goal / Possibility boundary

The accepted Goal definition remains:

```text
Goal
= persistent outcome/condition/change/pattern
  intentionally adopted as desired
```

The later accepted North Star requires LifeOS to work with candidate futures before that adoption exists. Possibility v0 therefore adds a bounded pre-commitment owner without broadening Goal.

Canonical separation:

```text
Possibility
= materially retained candidate future
  still open before intentional adoption

Goal
= intentionally adopted desired future/outcome/condition/pattern

Possibility != Goal
```

Natural-language interest does not mechanically establish Goal adoption:

```text
"maybe"
"one day"
"I could"
"that might be interesting"
"I would like to explore this"

!= Goal automatically
```

The material question remains whether the future has actually been adopted as intentionally desired in the applicable LifeOS context.

## Transition / history rule

A retained Possibility may later lead to a Goal, but Goal is not a status value of Possibility.

```text
T0 Possibility P1 retained
T1 explored
T2 intentional adoption occurs
T3 Goal G1 exists and may reference/derive from P1
```

Required history:

```text
T0–T1 != historical Goal pursuit
T2 does not rewrite P1 as though Goal adoption existed earlier
later abandonment of G1 != "G1 was never a Goal"
```

A later related Possibility may refer to historical P1/G1 without forcing identity equality.

## Discovery / AI boundary

```text
AI/system surfaces Possibility
!= user Goal

AI ranks candidate highly
!= Goal adoption

feasible candidate
!= Goal adoption
```

Only applicable intentional adoption establishes Goal semantics.

## Product-language boundary

`Idea`, `Aspiration`, `Someday` and `Maybe` remain contextual/product vocabulary. They do not weaken the Goal adoption invariant.

```text
vague aspiration retained pre-adoption
→ Possibility may apply

aspiration intentionally adopted as desired
→ Goal may apply
```
