# 2026-08-16 — Contribution versus Outcome

Contribution v0 preserves Outcome as shared result/disposition semantics while adding actor-scoped attribution around materially meaningful actual input/work.

```text
Outcome
= contextual result / disposition of Actual

Contribution
= actor-scoped materially meaningful actual input/work
  within a bounded realization/result/output context
```

Therefore:

```text
Outcome != Contribution
shared Outcome != duplicated actor-specific Outcomes
Contributor != owner/author of Outcome by default
Contribution != merit / blame for Outcome
```

Several Actors may hold different Contributions around one shared Outcome. A Contribution may be material without being sufficient, necessary or causally decisive for that Outcome; v0 does not introduce universal causal attribution.

Outcome correction and Contribution-attribution correction remain independently governed where the facts differ.
