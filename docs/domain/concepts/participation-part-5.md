# 2026-08-16 — Collective / Membership integration

Collective v0 resolves the old pressure between a true collective participant and several individual participants.

A true Collective may itself participate where the bounded occurrence/interaction semantics make the Collective the participant.

```text
Collective C
→ Participation in Event E
```

This is distinct from:

```text
Anna participates
Luca participates
Sara participates
```

Required boundaries:

```text
Collective Participation
!= every member individually participated

member Participation
!= Collective participated automatically

participant set
!= Collective automatically

Membership
!= Participation
```

Membership answers belonging to the Collective. Participation answers intended/response/Actual involvement in a bounded shared occurrence or interaction.

Therefore a member may decline an event, an external non-member may participate, and a Collective may be the participant even though individual member-level attendance is separately tracked or not material.

Changes to Membership do not rewrite historical Participation. Changes to Participation do not manufacture Membership or Collective identity.

The previously deferred group/collective-participant boundary is therefore **RESOLVED at the identity/role boundary** without introducing Team/Group/Member primitives.

Participation v0 remains **PASS WITH HARDENING; REOPEN = 0; UNCLASSIFIED = 0**.
