# 2026-08-16 — Contribution integration

Contribution v0 resolves the previously open boundary between involvement and materially meaningful actual contribution.

```text
Participation
= actor-specific involvement / intended-response-actual participation semantics

Contribution
= materially meaningful actual input/work attributable to an Actor
  within a bounded realization/result/output context
```

Therefore:

```text
Participation != Contribution
Participant != Contributor automatically
accepted participation != Contribution
attendance/presence != Contribution
response != Contribution
```

An Actor may participate without materially contributing. An Actor may also contribute material input in a way that is not exhausted by the final performer/attendance relation.

Contribution does not rewrite Participation history, and Participation does not establish Contribution by itself.

Several Participants do not imply identical Contributions. Several Contributors do not create a Collective automatically.
