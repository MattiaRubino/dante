# 2026-08-16 — Interpersonal Relationship integration

Person identity remains independent from contextual interpersonal relations.

Accepted new relation family:

```text
Interpersonal Relationship
SPECIFIC CONTEXTUAL PERSON↔PERSON RELATION FAMILY
```

A Person may therefore be related to another Person through materially useful interpersonal context such as parent/child, partner, friend or colleague while remaining the same native Person independently of that relation.

Examples:

```text
Person Maria
Interpersonal Relationship: mother of Mattia

Person Luca
Interpersonal Relationship: friend of Mattia
```

Canonical separation:

```text
Person != Interpersonal Relationship
Interpersonal Relationship != Person identity
```

The relation neither creates nor requires Account identity:

```text
Person B has no LifeOS Account
Interpersonal Relationship(A,B)
→ valid
```

Later Account creation, Account closure, provider linking or login-provider change does not itself create, transfer or erase the interpersonal relation.

## Relation versus contextual roles/capabilities

Interpersonal context does not manufacture operational/governance semantics:

```text
mother/friend/partner/colleague
!= Participant
!= Responsible Actor
!= Coordination Steward
!= Authority holder
!= Visibility recipient automatically
!= Consent giver for another purpose automatically
!= representative
!= contributor
```

Specific domain relations remain owners of those meanings.

## Collective boundary

Several Persons connected by interpersonal relations do not automatically form a Collective. A true Family/Household/Team Collective may separately exist where irreducible plurality has persistent identity/state/history/agency, with Membership modeled independently.

## History, evidence and privacy

Interpersonal relationship state may change without changing Person identity.

```text
current relationship != historical relationship
correction != silent overwrite
```

Provider/contact metadata and AI inference may support or propose a relationship through Evidence/Provenance but do not silently establish Person-to-Person relationship truth.

The existence and kind of an interpersonal relation may itself be sensitive. Visibility of the Person, relation kind, history and supporting Evidence/Provenance remains independently governed.

## Relationship-kind vocabulary

Relationship kinds are bounded/extensible vocabulary, not native identity and not one kernel primitive per label. No universal symmetry or transitivity is implied; inverse semantics belong to a specific kind where justified.
