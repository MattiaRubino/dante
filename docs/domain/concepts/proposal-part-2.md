# 2026-08-15 — Resource Allocation proposal boundary

Resource Requirement / Allocation v0 resolves the previously deferred resource-planning neighbor.

A Proposal may put forward:

- a candidate Resource provider;
- a Resource Allocation;
- a Requirement change;
- a capacity arrangement;
- another bounded resource-planning option.

But:

```text
Proposal for Allocation
!= Resource Allocation
```

The proposal remains a candidate semantic act until the affected resource-planning state becomes effective through the applicable family/policy/Authority semantics.

Likewise:

```text
proposed Requirement change
!= materially effective Requirement state
```

A prior Allocation does not silently carry to the proposed materially different Requirement state.

Proposal visibility, Allocation visibility and private eligibility/ranking basis remain independently governed.

AI may propose a candidate or Allocation. `AI proposal != effective Allocation` remains mandatory.

Proposal v0 remains **PASS WITH HARDENING, REOPEN = 0**.
