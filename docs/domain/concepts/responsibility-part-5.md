# 2026-08-15 — Responsibility fallback / Conditional Policy boundary

Responsibility v0 previously left Trigger/fallback/rotation policy as separately owned pressure.

Conditional Policy v0 resolves that boundary without changing Responsibility semantics.

```text
Responsibility
= who is accountable for ensuring a bounded commitment is appropriately handled

Conditional Policy
= what bounded response is intended when a qualifying basis is established
```

Therefore:

```text
Conditional Policy != Responsibility
Trigger != Responsibility transition
policy activation != effective Responsibility change
```


The affected Responsibility relation owns current/effective accountability state.

```text
Conditional Policy != Coordination Stewardship
Responsibility != Coordination Stewardship
```

Standalone Stewardship primitive status remains SAFE DEFERRED under its existing trigger/tests.

## Multi-Actor / Authority

A Conditional Policy can react to a Responsibility response/state without fabricating:

- Agreement or Consent;
- Acknowledgement;
- Participation;
- Authority;
- willingness;
- Actual performance.

Private reasons for a decline may remain private while a bounded fallback response is shared, subject to Visibility/Authority.

AI may propose a substitute or evaluate a fallback policy but does not transfer Responsibility or manufacture human acceptance.
