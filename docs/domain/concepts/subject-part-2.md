# 2026-08-16 — Collective integration

Subject remains the contextual aboutness role played by a native referent. Collective v0 adds one eligible native referent category:

```text
Collective
→ may play Subject role
```

This closes the previously deferred pressure between a true group/collective referent and several independent Subjects without creating a Subject wrapper identity.

Required distinction:

```text
Collective as Subject
!= each member individually having the same asserted state

several independent Subjects
!= Collective automatically
```

Example:

```text
Observation:
"The committee missed its response deadline"
Subject = Collective C
```

is not equivalent to asserting that every member individually missed a personal deadline.

Likewise:

```text
Subjects = Anna, Luca, Sara
```

does not by cardinality alone establish a Collective referent.

Membership remains independent:

```text
Subject != Membership
aboutness != belonging
```

Membership change does not rewrite the identity of the Collective Subject automatically. Where a later split/merge/replacement materially changes the Collective referent, historical Subject attribution remains tied to the referent/material state that actually applied.
