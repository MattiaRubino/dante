# 2026-08-16 — Content Artifact as possible Subject

Content Artifact / Document v0 introduces a scoped native information-content referent while preserving Subject as contextual aboutness role.

Canonical boundary:

```text
Content Artifact != Subject
```

A Content Artifact may play Subject role when an Observation, Evidence use, evaluation or another assertion is materially about that Artifact.

Examples:

```text
Observation about a document's page count
→ Artifact plays Subject role

Observation about the person described in that document
→ Person plays Subject role
not the Artifact merely because it contains the text
```

Content containment therefore does not make every mentioned referent identical to the Artifact's Subject role.

Artifact may be source material for structured assertions while the structured assertion keeps its own target/Subject semantics.

No universal `DocumentSubject` entity/root or embedded-content ontology is accepted.
