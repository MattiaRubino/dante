# 2026-08-16 — Interpersonal Relationship terminology

## Interpersonal Relationship

Use capitalized **Interpersonal Relationship** for the accepted specific contextual Person-to-Person relation family that preserves materially useful interpersonal context across LifeOS.

Canonical examples:

```text
Maria is Mattia's mother
Luca is Mattia's friend
Anna is Mattia's partner
Marco is Mattia's colleague
```

The term is deliberately narrower than generic `Relationship` and broader than any one kinship/social label.

```text
Interpersonal Relationship
!= universal Relationship root
!= social graph
!= Membership
!= Participation
!= Responsibility
!= Coordination Stewardship
!= Authority
!= Visibility
!= Agreement / Consent
!= Representation
!= Contribution
```

## Relationship kind

Use **relationship kind** as bounded/extensible vocabulary for the specific interpersonal meaning when useful.

Examples may include:

```text
parent / child
partner
friend
colleague
manager / report
caregiver / cared-for
client / adviser
teacher / student
```

Do not interpret this list as a closed taxonomy or separate kernel primitives/entities.

## UI / natural-language mapping

Ordinary UI may use natural vocabulary directly:

```text
Mother
Friend
Partner
Colleague
```

Natural-language phrases such as `my mother`, `my friend Luca`, `my partner` may resolve through an established authorized Interpersonal Relationship.

Do not silently normalize ambiguous phrases without adequate context/evidence.

## Rights and operational semantics

Do not map interpersonal labels directly to rights/duties:

```text
Mother → Authority       WRONG
Manager → Authority      WRONG without bounded governance semantics
Partner → Consent        WRONG
Friend → Visibility      WRONG
Caregiver → Responsibility/Representation automatically WRONG
```

Use the specific accepted relation/capability when those meanings are actually established.

## Direction / reciprocity language

Do not assume all interpersonal relations are symmetric or reciprocal. Parent/child may have explicit inverse vocabulary; friend/partner or other perspective-sensitive labels require the actual established semantics.

## Rejected kernel vocabulary

Do not introduce from this review:

```text
SocialGraph
RelationshipNode
RelationshipEdge
PersonalKnowledgeLink
Friend entity
Partner entity
Mother/Father entity
relationship strength score
intimacy score
relationship quality score
```

Specialist legal kinship, civil-status, HR hierarchy and regulated guardianship vocabulary remains outside the current kernel unless new product evidence justifies a dedicated review.
