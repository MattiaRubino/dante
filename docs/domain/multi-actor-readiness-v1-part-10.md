# 2026-08-16 — Contribution v0 multi-actor integration

Contribution v0 adds a specific actor-scoped relation for materially meaningful actual input/work without collapsing the already separated multi-actor dimensions.

```text
Contribution
!= Participation
!= Responsibility
!= Coordination Stewardship
!= Authority
!= Visibility
!= Consent
!= ownership
```

## Actor-scoped attribution

A shared realization/result can remain one fact while Contribution varies by Actor.

```text
shared Actual / Outcome
        ↓
Actor A → Contribution C1
Actor B → Contribution C2
Actor C → no established Contribution
```

This preserves the established shared-fact / actor-overlay discipline without making Contribution a universal overlay mechanism for unrelated semantics.

## Collective boundary

Several Contributors do not automatically form a Collective. A true Collective may bear Contribution where the contribution truthfully belongs to the Collective referent, while member-specific Contributions remain independent.

## Privacy and unequal power

Contribution attribution does not grant access to private supporting Evidence, Authority over another Actor, entitlement, credit or blame. A bounded shareable Contribution result may exist without disclosing private underlying causes/evidence where applicable.

## AI / automation

AI/system contribution is not inferred from technical generation, logging or provenance alone. It requires the accepted Actor semantics and explicit contextual applicability. Human Contribution, authorship, credit, acknowledgement or consent must never be fabricated from AI/system activity.
