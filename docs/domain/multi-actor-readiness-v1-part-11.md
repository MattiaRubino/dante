# 2026-08-16 — Ownership / Possession / Custody multi-actor integration

Ownership and Possession add two actor/referent-scoped relation families while preserving separation among multi-actor dimensions.

```text
Ownership
!= Responsibility
!= Coordination Stewardship
!= Authority
!= Visibility
!= Membership
!= Contribution

Possession
!= Responsibility
!= Coordination Stewardship
!= Authority
!= Resource Allocation
!= Actual use universally
```

Custody remains a bounded profile and must not become a shortcut for collapsing these dimensions.

## Multiple Actors

Several Actors may be owners or possessors where truthful. Multiplicity does not create Collective identity, equality, exclusivity or identical rights.

A true Collective may bear Ownership/Possession where attribution truthfully belongs to the Collective referent; member and Collective relations do not propagate automatically.

## Conflict and unequal power

Ownership/possession claims may conflict. LifeOS does not resolve them by newest source, creator, manager, provider, possessor, owner or AI confidence. Applicable Authority/Reconciliation/Evidence semantics remain separate.

Ownership/Possession also grant no universal disclosure entitlement over private Evidence or other Actors' information.

## AI / automation

AI may reason over authorized ownership/possession assertions but may not fabricate transfer, possession, custody, Authority or Visibility from recommendation/confidence alone.
