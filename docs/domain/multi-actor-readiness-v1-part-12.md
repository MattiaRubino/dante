# 2026-08-16 — Interpersonal Relationship multi-actor integration

Interpersonal Relationship adds persistent Person↔Person context while preserving all accepted multi-actor separations.

```text
Interpersonal Relationship
!= Membership
!= Participation
!= Responsibility
!= Coordination Stewardship
!= Authority
!= Visibility
!= Agreement / Consent
!= Representation
!= Contribution
```

## Perspective and reciprocity

One actor may establish or preserve an interpersonal relation from their own bounded context without manufacturing another actor's stance.

```text
A records B as friend
!= B automatically records A as friend
```

Specific inverse semantics may exist for a relationship kind when materially justified, but no universal reciprocity/symmetry/transitivity is accepted.

## Accountless and partial adoption

Both Persons may be represented without LifeOS Accounts. Person-related commitments and relationship-aware search therefore remain usable before full social/collaboration adoption.

## Collective boundary

Several interpersonally related Persons do not automatically become a Collective. A true Family/Household/Team Collective remains separately established where plurality has persistent irreducible identity/state/history/agency.

```text
member relationship != interpersonal relationship
interpersonal relationship != Membership
```

## Unequal power and governance

Labels such as parent, manager, caregiver, teacher or adviser do not automatically create:

```text
Authority
Visibility
Consent
Responsibility
Representation
```

Applicable specific relations/policies remain required.

## Privacy and inference

Person visibility, relationship existence, relationship kind, history and supporting Evidence may differ. Relationship-aware answers must not expose hidden relationship context or supporting Evidence to recipients lacking Visibility.

## Conflict / AI

Provider imports and AI may propose relationships but do not silently establish human relationship truth. Competing assertions may remain unresolved; no universal provider/newest/manager/AI-confidence winner.
