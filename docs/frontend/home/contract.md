## Historical Home job retained

Home was defined as a recurring orientation/operation surface combining:

```text
current situation
conversational access
temporal reality
carried/contextual information
direct action
secondary contextual support
```

It must not become a universal dashboard, ontology browser or AI-only cockpit.

The historical macro composition was:

```text
GLOBAL TOPBAR
ORIENTATION / CURRENT SITUATION
AI / CONVERSATIONAL SURFACE
CENTRAL STAGE
DAY / TIMELINE
RIGHT CONTEXT RAIL
```

Those ideas may continue to inform later B07 UI/UX consolidation, but they are not a license to override current semantic/application boundaries.
