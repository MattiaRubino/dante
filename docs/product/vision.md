# Product Vision

## Purpose

LifeOS helps a person convert long-term intentions into a sustainable daily plan, record what actually happened, and adapt the future plan when life changes.

## Core idea

The product is not only a calendar and not only a task manager. It connects:

1. goals and desired outcomes;
2. projects, programs, activities, and routines;
3. real calendar time;
4. user capacity and constraints;
5. confirmation of completed, partial, skipped, or unverified work;
6. gradual recalibration rather than constant destructive rescheduling.

## Platform direction

LifeOS is designed as one product available on:

- web browsers;
- Android;
- iOS.

Functionality should remain equivalent across platforms. Layout and interaction may adapt to the device instead of forcing pixel-identical interfaces.

## Product principles

- The user remains in control.
- Simple actions must remain simple.
- AI assists interpretation and planning but does not bypass validation.
- The system protects sustainable capacity, sleep, health, and free time.
- Optional modules can be enabled or disabled without damaging the core system.
- Data and infrastructure must remain portable.
